Welcome! I am a 4th-year Computer Science Ph.D. student at UC San Diego in the [Programming Systems](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/groups/progsys/) group in La Jolla, CA, advised by [Sorin Lerner](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~lerner/). I am extremely grateful for the additional mentorship of [Nikolaj Bjørner](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/nbjorner/) at Microsoft Research.

In summer 2026, I am joining the [Automated Reasoning Group](https://www.amazon.science/research-areas/automated-reasoning) at [Amazon Science](https://www.amazon.science/) as an Applied Scientist Intern. In summer 2025, I interned at [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/) in the [Research in Software Engineering (RiSE)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/research-software-engineering-rise/) group. Before coming to UCSD, I double majored in Computer Science and Music and minored in Mathematics at Pomona College in Claremont, CA, where I was advised by [Yuqing Melanie Wu](https://www.pomona.edu/directory/people/yuqing-melanie-wu) and [Ben Wiedermann](https://www.cs.hmc.edu/~benw/) in CS, and [Alfred Cramer](https://www.pomona.edu/directory/people/alfred-cramer) in Music. I used to work as a software engineer at [Meta](https://about.meta.com/), and as a freelance software engineer at [Stainless](https://www.stainless.com/).

My research interests lie at the intersection of Programming Languages, Automated Reasoning, and AI. I am interested in developing scalable automated reasoning techniques that support trustworthy, formally verified AI systems. I am particularly interested in improving the performance of SAT and SMT solvers. 

My recent work has centered on parallelization techniques for the SMT solver [Z3](https://github.com/z3prover/z3). My previous work combined stochastic and formal logic techniques to frame and solve the nested NP-hard combinatorial optimization problem of music structure summarization as an extension of the Generalized Median Graph problem. Check out [Projects](projects/) and [Publications](publications/) for more details!

I am also passionate about mentoring. I mentored four undergraduate students on a year-long research project in the 2025-2026 [UCSD Early Research Scholars Program](https://ersp.ucsd.edu) and served as Co-Chair for UCSD's [Graduate Women in Computing](https://gradwic.ucsd.edu/) organization in the 2024-2025 school year. In my free time, I am an amateur [flutist](https://www.youtube.com/@ilanashapiroflute), and I enjoy continuing my undergraduate musicology research on the Jewish Polish Soviet Holocaust-era composer Mieczysław Weinberg.
